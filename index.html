How it works

VAT is a small extra charge added to a price. In the Philippines it is 12%.

When the price you see already has VAT inside, it is called VAT-inclusive. This app finds the VAT part and the price without VAT.

Two easy steps

  1. Net of VAT (price without VAT): total ÷ 1.12
  2. Less 12% VAT: net of VAT × 12% (0.12)

Why 1.12? The price without VAT is 1 whole. VAT adds 0.12 more. So the total you pay is 1.12.

Example: ₱350

Step 1
350 ÷ 1.12 = 312.50
Net of VAT
Step 2
312.50 × 0.12 = 37.50
Less 12% VAT
Check
312.50 + 37.50 = 350.00
It adds back to the total.

What this app does not do

It only does the VAT math. It does not know if your item really has VAT. It also does not work out discounts or withholding tax. If you are not sure, ask your leader.

Calculation only. This app does not decide whether a sale is VATable, exempt, or zero-rated. Amounts stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.
